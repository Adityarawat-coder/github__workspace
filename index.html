Understanding Census 2027: What Every Citizen Needs to Know

Census 2027 is simple, safe, and directly benefits your family and local community.

1

What is Census 2027?

The official count of all people living in India conducted once every 10 years to determine how many hospitals, schools, roads, and drinking water pipelines are needed.

2

Why is it Important?

Ensures your family gets government welfare benefits including Ration cards, Ayushman Bharat health coverage, PM Awas housing, and subsidized electricity.

3

How to Complete it?

1. Enter Aadhaar & Mobile OTP.
2. Add family members and basic house details.
3. Receive your Digital Reference Pass to show when the officer visits.

4

Is Your Data Safe?

100% Confidential under Census Act 1948 Section 15. Individual records are never shared with police, courts, or tax authorities.

Statutory Protection

Security, Privacy & Data Protection Framework

Comprehensive legal safeguards under Census Act 1948 (Section 15), 256-bit AES cryptographic encryption, and DPDP Act 2023 compliance.

Section 15 of Census Act, 1948 (Statutory Secrecy)

Under Section 15 of the Census Act, no person has a right to inspect any book, register, or record of census returns. No court, police officer, or tax authority can require any census officer or citizen to produce census answers as evidence in any judicial or administrative proceeding.

256-Bit AES Cryptography & TLS 1.3

All digital census submissions are protected with Military-Grade AES-256-GCM encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Data is stored on sovereign government servers inside Tier-IV National Informatics Centre (NIC) MeghRaj Cloud.

Zero Biometric Storage (UIDAI Tokenized)

Aadhaar authentication is strictly ephemeral and tokenized through UIDAI-certified e-KYC channels. No raw biometric data (fingerprint or iris) is ever captured or stored in the census database.

DPDP Act 2023 & Purpose Limitation

Complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Information collected is used exclusively for statistical aggregation and national planning. Individual records are never sold, monetized, or shared with commercial entities.
